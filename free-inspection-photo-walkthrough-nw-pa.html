What a Free Exterior Inspection Looks Like: 5 Steps in Real Photos.

People ask me what actually happens when we come out. So here's a real one, start to finish, with the photos we took. The owner said we could share them. We left out anything that identifies the property.

Published November 11, 2026 by Bret Ruth, TableTurn Digital. General information only. It does not diagnose a specific home or determine insurance coverage.

1. Walk the whole property

We start on the ground and walk every side of every building, not just the front of the house. Storms are driven by wind, so one wall can take most of the hits while the others look fine. On this property that meant more than one building.

2. The roof and the vents

Next is the roof itself: the shingles, the ridge and the metal vents. Soft metal like vents and caps tends to show hits before shingles do, so it's one of the first places we look.

3. Gutters and downspouts

We check the front edge of the gutters and the length of each downspout for dents, and look at what washed out at the bottom. Dark, sandy grit there can be granules off the shingles above.

4. Siding, up close

Then we get close to the siding. On this inspection the vinyl had holes, cracks and dents like the one in this photo. They're easy to walk past from the driveway and easy to see from three feet away.

5. Photos of everything

Everything we find gets photographed, and we walk you through the photos in plain language. If we don't find anything, we tell you that too. There's no pressure and no same day contract. We document the damage. Your insurance company decides what your policy covers.

Common questions

How long does a free exterior inspection take?

Most homes take well under an hour. It depends on the size of the house, how many buildings are on the property and what we find.

Do I need to be home for the inspection?

It helps. We like to ask what you've noticed and walk you through the photos at the end, but we can work around your schedule.

Will you tell me if there's no damage?

Yes. If we don't find anything, we tell you that and show you the photos. There's no pressure and no same day contract.

Does the inspection decide my insurance claim?

No. We document what we find. Your insurance company decides what your policy covers.
